## Trust levels

| Level | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Checked against the official text | A binding text or official publication was read in its official form or a faithful copy |
| Official text cited, checked through reliable reports | The binding text is identified (with its Journal officiel issue when known) but was read through press or guides; the official PDF still needs opening |
| No official text; confirmed by real examples | Nothing official is published; the rule reproduces real documents |
